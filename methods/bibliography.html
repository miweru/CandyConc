Bibliography#

The methods pages cite the following works. Each entry was checked against the publisher, Crossref, the ACL Anthology, or the author's publication page. For the central definitions (Log Ratio in Hardie 2014, the conservative Log Ratio in Evert 2022, logDice in Rychlý 2008, DP in Gries 2008, and the contingency table for distance-based co-occurrence in Evert 2004), the definition was read in the full text. Where a formula was taken from a secondary source, the methods page says so. Stephanie Evert published under the name Stefan Evert until 2011.

Benjamini, Yoav, and Yosef Hochberg. 1995. Controlling the false discovery rate: A practical and powerful approach to multiple testing. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B 57(1), pages 289 to 300. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x

Bouma, Gerlof. 2009. Normalized (pointwise) mutual information in collocation extraction. In Christian Chiarcos, Richard Eckart de Castilho, and Manfred Stede (eds.), Von der Form zur Bedeutung: Proceedings of the Biennial GSCL Conference 2009, pages 31 to 40.

Carroll, John B. 1970. An alternative to Juilland's usage coefficient for lexical frequencies and a proposal for a standard frequency index. Computer Studies in the Humanities and Verbal Behavior 3(2), pages 61 to 65.

Church, Kenneth W., and Patrick Hanks. 1990. Word association norms, mutual information, and lexicography. Computational Linguistics 16(1), pages 22 to 29. https://aclanthology.org/J90-1003/

Church, Kenneth, William Gale, Patrick Hanks, and Donald Hindle. 1991. Using statistics in lexical analysis. In Uri Zernik (ed.), Lexical Acquisition, pages 115 to 164. Hillsdale: Lawrence Erlbaum.

Clopper, C. J., and E. S. Pearson. 1934. The use of confidence or fiducial limits illustrated in the case of the binomial. Biometrika 26(4), pages 404 to 413. https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/26.4.404

Cochran, William G. 1954. Some methods for strengthening the common χ² tests. Biometrics 10(4), pages 417 to 451. https://doi.org/10.2307/3001616

Cohen, Jacob. 1960. A coefficient of agreement for nominal scales. Educational and Psychological Measurement 20(1), pages 37 to 46. https://doi.org/10.1177/001316446002000104

Covington, Michael A., and Joe D. McFall. 2010. Cutting the Gordian knot: The moving-average type-token ratio (MATTR). Journal of Quantitative Linguistics 17(2), pages 94 to 100. https://doi.org/10.1080/09296171003643098

Daille, Béatrice. 1994. Approche mixte pour l'extraction de terminologie: statistique lexicale et filtres linguistiques. Doctoral dissertation, Université Paris 7. https://theses.fr/1994PA077353

Dice, Lee R. 1945. Measures of the amount of ecologic association between species. Ecology 26(3), pages 297 to 302. https://doi.org/10.2307/1932409

Dunn, Olive Jean. 1961. Multiple comparisons among means. Journal of the American Statistical Association 56(293), pages 52 to 64. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1961.10482090

Dunning, Ted. 1993. Accurate methods for the statistics of surprise and coincidence. Computational Linguistics 19(1), pages 61 to 74. https://aclanthology.org/J93-1003/

Ellis, Nick C. 2006. Language acquisition as rational contingency learning. Applied Linguistics 27(1), pages 1 to 24. https://doi.org/10.1093/applin/ami038

Evert, Stefan. 2004. The Statistics of Word Cooccurrences: Word Pairs and Collocations. Doctoral dissertation, Universität Stuttgart, published 2005. URN urn:nbn:de:bsz:93-opus-23714. https://www.stephanie-evert.de/PUB/Evert2004phd.pdf

Evert, Stefan. 2009. Corpora and collocations. In Anke Lüdeling and Merja Kytö (eds.), Corpus Linguistics: An International Handbook, volume 2, pages 1212 to 1248. Berlin: Mouton de Gruyter. https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110213881.2.1212 Often cited as 2008.

Evert, Stefan, and Andrew Hardie. 2011. Twenty-first century Corpus Workbench: Updating a query architecture for the new millennium. Proceedings of the Corpus Linguistics 2011 Conference, Birmingham. https://www.stephanie-evert.de/PUB/EvertHardie2011.pdf

Evert, Stephanie. 2022. Measuring keyness. Digital Humanities 2022: Conference Abstracts, pages 202 to 205. Tokyo: DH2022 Local Organizing Committee. https://www.stephanie-evert.de/PUB/Evert2022.pdf Replication material by Stephanie Evert and Jan Majewski (2022): https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/CY6MW

Fleiss, Joseph L. 1971. Measuring nominal scale agreement among many raters. Psychological Bulletin 76(5), pages 378 to 382.

Gries, Stefan Th. 2008. Dispersions and adjusted frequencies in corpora. International Journal of Corpus Linguistics 13(4), pages 403 to 437. https://doi.org/10.1075/ijcl.13.4.02gri

Gries, Stefan Th. 2013. 50-something years of work on collocations: What is or should be next. International Journal of Corpus Linguistics 18(1), pages 137 to 165. https://doi.org/10.1075/ijcl.18.1.09gri

Gries, Stefan Th. 2020. Analyzing dispersion. In Magali Paquot and Stefan Th. Gries (eds.), A Practical Handbook of Corpus Linguistics, pages 99 to 118. Cham: Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-46216-1_5

Guiraud, Pierre. 1954. Les caractères statistiques du vocabulaire. Paris: Presses Universitaires de France.

Hardie, Andrew. 2014. Log Ratio: an informal introduction. Blog of the ESRC Centre for Corpus Approaches to Social Science (CASS), Lancaster University, 28 April 2014. https://cass.lancs.ac.uk/log-ratio-an-informal-introduction/

Heinrich, Philipp, and Stephanie Evert. 2024. Operationalising the Hermeneutic Grouping Process in Corpus-assisted Discourse Studies. In Proceedings of the 4th Workshop on Computational Linguistics for the Political and Social Sciences (CPSS), Vienna, pages 33 to 44. https://aclanthology.org/2024.cpss-1.3/ The source code of CandyConc names this publication as the reference for the exact conditional computation of the conservative Log Ratio.

Johnson, Jeff, Matthijs Douze, and Hervé Jégou. 2021. Billion-scale similarity search with GPUs. IEEE Transactions on Big Data 7(3), pages 535 to 547. https://doi.org/10.1109/TBDATA.2019.2921572

Juilland, Alphonse, and Eugenio Chang-Rodríguez. 1964. Frequency Dictionary of Spanish Words. The Hague: Mouton.

Katz, D., J. Baptista, S. P. Azen, and M. C. Pike. 1978. Obtaining confidence intervals for the risk ratio in cohort studies. Biometrics 34(3), pages 469 to 474. https://doi.org/10.2307/2530610

Kilgarriff, Adam, Pavel Rychlý, Pavel Smrž, and David Tugwell. 2004. The Sketch Engine. Proceedings of the 11th EURALEX International Congress, Lorient, pages 105 to 115.

Lijffijt, Jefrey, and Stefan Th. Gries. 2012. Correction to Stefan Th. Gries, "Dispersions and adjusted frequencies in corpora". International Journal of Corpus Linguistics 17(1), pages 147 to 149. https://doi.org/10.1075/ijcl.17.1.08lij

Pearson, Karl. 1900. On the criterion that a given system of deviations from the probable in the case of a correlated system of variables is such that it can be reasonably supposed to have arisen from random sampling. Philosophical Magazine, Series 5, 50(302), pages 157 to 175. https://doi.org/10.1080/14786440009463897

Rayson, Paul, and Roger Garside. 2000. Comparing corpora using frequency profiling. Proceedings of the Workshop on Comparing Corpora, pages 1 to 6. https://aclanthology.org/W00-0901/

Rychlý, Pavel. 2008. A lexicographer-friendly association score. Proceedings of Recent Advances in Slavonic Natural Language Processing (RASLAN) 2008, pages 6 to 9. Brno: Masaryk University. https://nlp.fi.muni.cz/raslan/2008/papers/13.pdf

Scott, Mike. WordSmith Tools, help page "Type/token ratios". https://lexically.net/downloads/version6/HTML/type_token_ratio_proc.htm

Wilson, Andrew. 2013. Embracing Bayes factors for key item analysis in corpus linguistics. In Markus Bieswanger and Amei Koll-Stobbe (eds.), New Approaches to the Study of Linguistic Variability, pages 3 to 11. Frankfurt: Peter Lang. https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/51045/

Wilson, Edwin B. 1927. Probable inference, the law of succession, and statistical inference. Journal of the American Statistical Association 22(158), pages 209 to 212. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1927.10502953
