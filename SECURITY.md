# Security policy

Security fixes are released in the latest version of CandyConc. Include the
version from `candy --version` when reporting a vulnerability.

## Report a vulnerability

Use [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/miweru/CandyConc/security/advisories/new)
to contact the maintainer privately. If that form is unavailable, email
[michael.ruppert@fau.de](mailto:michael.ruppert@fau.de).

Describe the affected feature, the steps to reproduce the problem, its
impact, and your operating system. A small synthetic example is useful.
Remove credentials, personal data, and confidential corpus text from logs
and examples. Keep vulnerability details out of public issues while the
report is being investigated.

The maintainer will discuss reproduction, a fix, and disclosure with you
through the private report. Ordinary bugs belong in the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/miweru/CandyConc/issues).
