# Code of conduct

CandyConc welcomes researchers, developers, and users of all backgrounds
and levels of experience.

## Working together

- Discuss ideas and results with respect. Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Give constructive feedback and make room for questions from new users.
- Respect privacy and the permissions attached to other people's data.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and the publication
  of private information without permission are unacceptable.

These rules apply to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and interactions in which someone represents CandyConc.

## Reporting and moderation

Report a concern privately to Michael Ruppert at
[michael.ruppert@fau.de](mailto:michael.ruppert@fau.de). Include links or
examples where possible. Reports will be handled discreetly, with information
shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict
participation according to the severity and recurrence of the conduct.
Decisions should explain which conduct prompted the action and what is
expected next. Questions about a decision can be sent to the same address.
