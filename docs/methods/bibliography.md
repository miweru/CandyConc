# Bibliography

The methods pages cite the following works. Each entry was checked against the
publisher, Crossref, the ACL Anthology, or the author's publication page. For
the central definitions (Log Ratio in Hardie 2014, the conservative Log Ratio
in Evert 2022, logDice in Rychlý 2008, DP in Gries 2008, and the contingency
table for distance-based co-occurrence in Evert 2004), the definition was read
in the full text. Where a formula was taken from a secondary source, the
methods page says so. Stephanie Evert published under the name Stefan Evert
until 2011.

Benjamini, Yoav, and Yosef Hochberg. 1995. Controlling the false discovery
rate: A practical and powerful approach to multiple testing. *Journal of the
Royal Statistical Society, Series B* 57(1), pages 289 to 300.
<https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x>

Bouma, Gerlof. 2009. Normalized (pointwise) mutual information in collocation
extraction. In Christian Chiarcos, Richard Eckart de Castilho, and Manfred
Stede (eds.), *Von der Form zur Bedeutung: Proceedings of the Biennial GSCL
Conference 2009*, pages 31 to 40.

Carroll, John B. 1970. An alternative to Juilland's usage coefficient for
lexical frequencies and a proposal for a standard frequency index. *Computer
Studies in the Humanities and Verbal Behavior* 3(2), pages 61 to 65.

Church, Kenneth W., and Patrick Hanks. 1990. Word association norms, mutual
information, and lexicography. *Computational Linguistics* 16(1), pages 22
to 29. <https://aclanthology.org/J90-1003/>

Church, Kenneth, William Gale, Patrick Hanks, and Donald Hindle. 1991. Using
statistics in lexical analysis. In Uri Zernik (ed.), *Lexical Acquisition*,
pages 115 to 164. Hillsdale: Lawrence Erlbaum.

Clopper, C. J., and E. S. Pearson. 1934. The use of confidence or fiducial
limits illustrated in the case of the binomial. *Biometrika* 26(4), pages 404
to 413. <https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/26.4.404>

<https://doi.org/10.1177/001316446002000104>

Daille, Béatrice. 1994. *Approche mixte pour l'extraction de terminologie:
